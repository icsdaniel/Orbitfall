# Design review, 2026-10-26

Fresh review of PLAN.md, DESIGN.md, CLAUDE.md, the plan doc, project memory, the project chat and the planning thread. Result: `BRIEF.md`, a self-contained build handoff for M0 and M1.

## What is strong

- **A clear north star with teeth.** "Feels like a tower defence first", M1 as a small complete TD, and a feel gate Daniele plays at every milestone. This is the best protection the project has against scope creep.
- **One theme that is also the economy.** Chase the light ties income (panels), threat (Eclipser shadows), the win condition (enemy planets dim the sun) and even a tower (Sun Lens) into one problem: where is the light and who is blocking it.
- **Rotation has real reasons.** Panels vs targets in M1, then shields and weak points in M2. Each reason is introduced one milestone at a time, which limits overload.
- **Fair-play rules.** No hard counters (25% damage floor), at least three answers per threat, and a forecast that reads the same data as the spawner, so it can't lie.
- **Agent-friendly engineering.** Deterministic C++ core testable without Godot, content as JSON, every PR playable from a phone link, and bots to check balance questions.

## Problems found and how they were fixed

| # | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No numbers anywhere.** An agent building M1 would have to invent every cost, speed, HP and wave | BRIEF §7.2: starter values for the planet, sun, 3 buildings, 3 enemies and 8 waves, all in data |
| 2 | **Light and shadow had no maths.** "Thin beam" vs "about 90° arc" vs "planet size sets lit fraction" didn't fit together | Beam = band of parallel rays of fixed width, so lit arc = 2·asin(W/2R) and bigger planets naturally get less light. Exact shadow test per caster written down (BRIEF §5) |
| 3 | **Weak points couldn't be hit.** A weak side "facing a world bearing" is unreachable: shots from the planet arrive almost straight out at long range | A weak-point hit = the firing tower sits within ±8° of the enemy's bearing (directly under it). Same intent: rotate to line up the Lance |
| 4 | **M1 forecast showed an enemy planet source**, but M1 has no enemy planets | M1 shows the lane bearing; enemy-planet source arrives in M4 |
| 5 | **M1 had one lane**, so rotation and the forecast's bearing column barely mattered | Two lanes 140° apart; with the drifting sun this gives a decision every wave |
| 6 | **"Slow time 25%" was ambiguous** (25% speed or 25% slower?) | 75% speed by default, a tunable data value. Daniele's words were "slow time by a percentage" |
| 7 | **Intermission undefined:** does the sun move, does income tick? | Sim is frozen between waves; you can rotate, build and sell; Start Wave resumes |
| 8 | **Eclipser behaviour undefined** beyond "drifts between sun and planet" | Parks on the sun line over your lit panels, follows the sun, then descends and lands if not killed |
| 9 | **Satellites had HP and could be destroyed**, against the never-damaged rule | Default: satellites are jammed (offline for a while), never destroyed. Flagged for confirmation (DESIGN Q32) |
| 10 | **M0 carried scale targets** (1M pool ops, 200k instances) before anything was playable | Moved to M3; M0 now aims at 20k instances so the path to M1 is short |
| 11 | **Web threading via PWA isolation** was fragile on GitHub Pages | Use Godot's no-threads web template (4.3+); the core already has a single-threaded mode |
| 12 | Stale and duplicate notes: two Q22 entries in DESIGN.md, open-items list missing Q23/Q27/Q30, CLAUDE.md not pointing at a handoff | Renumbered, lists updated, CLAUDE.md now reads BRIEF.md first |
| 13 | "Three answers per threat" can't be satisfied with M1's 2 towers | Rule enforced from M2 when content exists |

## Not changed, worth watching

- **M0 is still heavy** (C++ extension, wasm, Pages per PR, APK) before anything is fun. The design toys (D2 to D4) are the safety valve: they run in plain GDScript as soon as the link pipeline works.
- **Shields lost stakes** when buildings became undamageable; the tension is uptime only. Check at the M2 gate.
- **150k entities at 60 fps on a mid-range phone in Godot** is ambitious (about 7 MB of instance data per frame). M3.6 reports the bottlenecks if it misses.
- **The plan doc** (claude.ai artifact) is now behind the files; the files in `plan/` are the source of truth.
